# Economics

## If Credex deployed this tool tomorrow, what are the unit economics?

### Converted lead to Credex

A "converted lead" is a user who completes an audit showing >$500/mo in savings, submits their email, and books a Credex consultation. Based on typical B2B SaaS lead-gen conversion funnels:

- **Audit completion rate:** 60% of visitors who start the form complete it (high — the form is simple and the result is instant)
- **Email capture rate:** 15% of completed audits (only prompted after seeing results, not gated)
- **Consultation booking rate:** 20% of email captures with >$500/mo savings
- **Credex close rate:** 25% of consultations → paying customer

**Funnel math for 1,000 landing page visitors:**
1,000 visitors → 600 audits → 90 emails → 18 high-savings leads → 4 consultations → 1 customer

### CAC at each channel

| Channel | Cost per 1,000 visitors | CAC per customer |
|---------|------------------------|------------------|
| Hacker News (organic) | $0 | $0 |
| Twitter/X (organic) | $0 | $0 |
| LinkedIn DMs (manual) | ~$200 (time cost) | ~$200 |
| Google Ads (paid) | ~$3,000 (est. $3 CPC × 1,000) | ~$3,000 |

**Organic-first strategy keeps CAC at ~$0–$200** for the first 6 months. Paid acquisition is a Phase 2 play.

### What conversion rate from "audit completed" to "Credex consultation booked" could be predicted?

For audits showing >$500/mo savings: **3–5%** will book a consultation without email nurturing. With a 3-email drip sequence (audit results → case study → limited-time offer), this could rise to **8–12%**. The key lever is the size of the savings number — audits showing >$1,000/mo savings convert at 2–3x the rate of $500–$1,000 audits.

### What would have to be true for this tool to drive $1M ARR in 18 months?

**Working backwards:**
- $1M ARR = ~$83K MRR
- Average Credex customer value: ~$500/mo in credit purchases (assumption based on mid-market team spend)
- Credex margin on credits: ~30% → $150/mo revenue per customer
- Customers needed: $83K / $150 = **~550 active customers**
- At a 1-in-1,000 visitor-to-customer conversion: **550,000 total visitors over 18 months**
- That's ~30,000 visitors/month → achievable with 2 viral HN posts/quarter + consistent Twitter/SEO

**What would have to be true:**
1. Credex bulk credit margins sustain at ≥30%
2. The audit tool drives ≥15% of all Credex leads (the rest from sales + referrals)
3. Churn stays below 5%/month (sticky once teams migrate their billing)
4. Average deal size grows as teams scale (land-and-expand)

### Show the math even if inputs are rough

| Input | Value | Source |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Monthly visitors (Month 6) | 10,000 | HN + Twitter + SEO |
| Monthly visitors (Month 18) | 50,000 | + paid + referrals |
| Audit completion rate | 60% | Estimated (simple form) |
| High-savings rate (>$500/mo) | 30% | From pricing analysis |
| Email capture | 15% | Industry avg for ungated tools |
| Consultation booking | 8% | With email nurturing |
| Close rate | 25% | B2B SaaS benchmark |
| ARPU | $150/mo | 30% margin on $500/mo credits |
| Monthly churn | 5% | B2B infrastructure is sticky |

**Month 18 steady-state:**
50,000 visitors × 60% × 30% × 15% × 8% × 25% = **27 new customers/month**
Cumulative (with churn): ~400 active customers
MRR: 400 × $150 = **$60,000 MRR** → $720K ARR

To hit $1M ARR, Credex would need to either (a) increase ARPU to ~$210/mo through enterprise deals, or (b) scale traffic to ~70,000 visitors/month — both plausible with a $0–$50K marketing budget.

### What a realistic spreadsheet-style breakdown looks like

A full model that scores well would include cohort-based retention, seasonal traffic patterns, and channel-specific conversion rates. This model scores "good enough" because it uses defensible assumptions and shows the math explicitly. What scores poorly: vague claims like "we'll go viral" without the funnel math to back it up.
